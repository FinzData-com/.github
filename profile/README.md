## New: survivorship-free US stock data for Qlib and RD-Agent

A ready Qlib data folder in one call: every listed and delisted US stock that traded on the NYSE and Nasdaq since 2000, split- and dividend-adjusted, with point-in-time top 500, 1,000 and 3,000 universes. Starter kit: **[qlib-finzdata](https://github.com/FinzData-com/qlib-finzdata)** · Guide: [finzdata.com/qlib](https://finzdata.com/qlib)

## Everything FinzData covers

Every one of the 16,000+ US stocks that has traded on the NYSE and Nasdaq in the last 26+ years, delisted included, point in time. Around them: 139 futures markets with every contract since 2007, the VIX and Cboe volatility indexes with the VIX futures curve, options-market gamma for single stocks, ETFs and the major indexes, 5,100+ ETFs, and 512 crypto pairs with Bitcoin since 2013.

Macro exactly as it was known: 22,700+ series including CPI, payrolls, wages, GDP and the Treasury yield curve, every vintage kept. Plus COT positioning since 1986, 79 FX pairs, 123 indexes, and the real economy as it happens: daily ship traffic through 28 chokepoints and 2,000+ ports, flights by airport and airline, toll and congestion traffic, and electricity use and power demand by US state and for 87 countries.

Bars at every resolution you backtest on: tick, 1-minute, 5-minute, 15-minute, 30-minute, hourly and daily, with tick-level trades on the Institutional plan. In pandas with one line:

```
pip install finzdata
```
